Raynova Smart Solar Street Lighting Project

Impact 360

Raynova

Impact 360 is a sustainable initiative by Raynova that powers street lights using solar energy while replacing conventional bulbs with energy-efficient LED bulbs. The project helps reduce electricity costs, promotes renewable energy, and creates safer, well-lit communities.

About the Project

Cleaner lighting for safer roads

The purpose of Impact 360 is to create a reliable street lighting system that reduces the pressure on the electricity grid and supports safer public spaces after sunset.

The Problem

Traditional street lights can waste energy and create high electricity costs for communities.

The Alternative

Solar energy is renewable, locally available during the day, and ideal for powering efficient LED lighting.

Objectives

What Impact 360 aims to achieve

Reduce electricity consumption.
Promote renewable energy.
Replace old bulbs with LED bulbs.
Improve road safety with reliable street lighting.
Support sustainable community development.

How It Works

From sunlight to street light

1

Solar Collection

Solar panels collect sunlight during the day.

2

Power Control

The charge controller regulates the power.

3

Battery Storage

The battery stores the generated electricity.

4

Automatic Lighting

At night, the battery powers the LED street lights automatically.

Benefits

Practical gains for the community

Impact 360 combines renewable power with long-lasting LED bulbs to make public lighting more affordable, dependable, and environmentally responsible.

  • Saves electricity costs.
  • Environmentally friendly.
  • Low maintenance.
  • Reliable during power outages.
  • Long lifespan of LED lights.
  • Reduces carbon emissions.
